WHAT WE OFFER

Our Cleaning Services

Choose the cleaning service that suits your property, schedule, and priorities.

Regular Cleaning

Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly cleaning to help keep your home fresh, tidy, and comfortable. Our service includes kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and general tidying.

One-off Cleaning

A convenient home refresh when you need extra help without committing to a regular cleaning schedule. Ideal for getting your home back to a clean and comfortable condition.

Deep Cleaning

A more detailed clean for areas that need extra attention. We focus on kitchens, bathrooms, surfaces, floors, and other accessible areas to help remove built-up dirt, dust, and grime.

Airbnb & Short-Stay Cleaning

Reliable turnover cleaning between guests, including bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, and agreed restocking. We can also handle pre-check-in item pickups, sourcing and replacing missing essentials such as bed sheets, kitchen plates, toiletries, and other agreed supplies.

End of Lease Cleaning

Thorough end of lease cleaning to help leave your property fresh, spotless, and ready for inspection. We focus on kitchens, bathrooms, floors, bedrooms, and other key areas to help tenants prepare their property for handover.

Small Office Cleaning

Reliable and consistent cleaning for small offices and local businesses across Melbourne. We keep workspaces, kitchens, bathrooms, and common areas clean, fresh, and presentable, so you can focus on running your business.
