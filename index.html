OUR SERVICES

Professional Cleaning Solutions

From regular upkeep to detailed property preparation, choose the service that fits your needs.

Regular Cleaning

Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly cleaning to help keep your home fresh, tidy, and comfortable. Our service includes kitchens, bathrooms, dusting, vacuuming, mopping, and general tidying.

One-off Cleaning

A convenient home refresh when you need some extra help without committing to a regular cleaning schedule. Ideal for getting your home back to a clean and comfortable condition.

Deep Cleaning

A more detailed clean for areas that need extra attention. We focus on kitchens, bathrooms, surfaces, floors, and other accessible areas to help remove built-up dirt, dust, and grime.

Airbnb & Short-Stay Cleaning

Reliable turnover cleaning between guests, including bedrooms, bathrooms, kitchens, and agreed restocking. We also handle pre-check-in item pickups—sourcing and replacing any missing essentials like new bed sheets, kitchen plates, toiletries, or extra supplies needed for a seamless stay.

End of Lease Cleaning

Thorough end of lease cleaning to help leave your property fresh, spotless, and ready for inspection. We focus on kitchens, bathrooms, floors, bedrooms, and other key areas, helping tenants make a great final impression and prepare their property for handover.

Small Office Cleaning

Reliable and consistent cleaning for small offices and local businesses across Melbourne. We keep workspaces, kitchens, bathrooms, and common areas clean, fresh, and presentable, so you can focus on running your business.
